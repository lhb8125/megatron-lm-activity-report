## Overview

Delivered work concentrated on Megatron-FSDP, generalized tensor parallelism, THD sequence packing, dynamic and disaggregated inference, hybrid state-space models, MoE execution, CUDA Graph reliability, RL, multimodal training, and sparse attention. ### MoE and transformer decode-path kernels

The dynamic MoE decode path is being optimized through fused routing, expert GEMM epilogues, reduced launch counts, stable CUDA Graph buffers, alternative attention backends, and new fused MoE implementations.

- Decode routing combines softmax and Top-K selection, builds expert indirection in one kernel, and eliminates separate padding and activation launches.
- Expert FC1 and FC2 paths gain dedicated tiling, fused SwiGLU epilogues, BF16 combine buffers, and retuned activation and token-count grids.
